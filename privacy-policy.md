## 2. Camera and Image Data

The App requests camera access so you can scan QR codes and barcodes using your device camera.

Camera frames are used to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes. The App does not require camera images to be uploaded to our own server for the core scanning feature.

If you choose to scan an image from your device, the App may access that selected image to detect supported codes. The selected image is used for the feature you requested.
